# WOW-284 v2.1.0

This release publishes the reader-focused fourteen-page research note
*Exact Counterexamples and Spectral Mechanisms for WOW-284* together with its
reproducibility archive.

Highlights:

- a self-contained 50-vertex Hoffman--Singleton counterexample and the sharp
  degree threshold for diameter-two Moore graphs;
- exact counterexamples of orders 38, 39, 40, and 42;
- exhaustive exact verification of the labelled singleton- and edge-deletion
  descendant families;
- a regular diameter-three spectral criterion;
- Lean 4.31 verification of the complete 50-vertex certificate and finite
  spectral certificates for orders 38, 39, 40, and 42;
- ten indexed post-manuscript proof audits with independent exact verifiers;
- synchronized TeX, Markdown, PDF, arXiv source, citation metadata, and
  SHA-256 integrity records.

This is the reproducibility release corresponding to the manuscript.
