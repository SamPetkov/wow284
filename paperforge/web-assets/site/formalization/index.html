Formalization guide

The repository contains three distinct formal layers: a complete graph-level certificate for the 50-vertex Hoffman–Singleton counterexample, finite matrix certificates for the smaller examples, and a graph-independent formalization of the exact nonbacktracking LP optimum and its rigidity. The distinctions below are part of the mathematical claim.

1. Complete 50-vertex development

The default Wow284 library defines the coordinate graph on fifty vertices and checks symmetry, irreflexivity, degree seven, the common-neighbour rule, diameter two, girth five, the adjacency-square and distance identities, and an exact rational diagonalization. The endpoint therefore verifies the least distance eigenvalue and the strict WOW-284 gap at graph level.

Structure

Wow284.Adjacent, Wow284.degree_seven, and the exhaustive common-neighbour certificate.

The distance spectrum is verified as \(91^{(1)},(-4)^{(28)},1^{(21)}\) using rational matrix identities.

Moore obstruction

Wow284.moore_wow_bound_iff formalizes the exact degree threshold in the Moore-graph family.

2. Finite certificates at orders 38, 39, 40, and 42

These endpoints certify explicit finite matrices and exact dual-degree data. The order-40 development includes a complete invertible diagonalization. Orders 38, 39, and 42 use exact shifted positive-definiteness certificates sufficient for strict failure. Their scope is intentionally described as a kernel-checked finite spectral certificate rather than a single end-to-end theorem phrased through Mathlib’s generic graph-distance API.

3. Exact all-degree LP optimum

The separate analytic development formalizes the one-variable, all-degree two-sided nonbacktracking linear program. For every integer \(k\ge4\), it constructs the normalized extremal coefficient family, proves admissibility and attainment, establishes finite-support weak duality and the universal Chebyshev tail, and identifies all equality cases both polynomially and coefficientwise.

Lean endpoint

Wow284.LP.twoSidedLP_exact_optimum_and_coefficient_rigidity

The theorem is graph-independent: it proves the optimization statement but does not formalize the later graph trace bridge, integral-excess theorem, puncture spectra, or deletion theory.

Trust boundary

Included in the Lean claim

  • The explicit fifty-vertex graph and exact spectrum
  • Finite spectral endpoints at orders 38, 39, 40, and 42
  • The nonbacktracking recurrence and exact LP optimum
  • Attainment, weak duality, complementary slackness, and optimizer rigidity

Proved outside that Lean claim

  • The graph trace interpretation of the LP polynomials
  • The integral optimal-slack and three-to-one theorems
  • Punctured-Moore spectra and deletion stability
  • Finite Python classifications explicitly identified in the paper

The release-static axiom reports for representative endpoints contain only standard Mathlib foundations such as propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. The public source rejects sorry, admit, native_decide, bv_decide, unsafe declarations, and project axioms in imported roots.
