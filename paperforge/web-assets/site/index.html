Counterexamples, Spectral Obstructions, and Deletion Stability for WOW-284

Samuil Petkov · École normale supérieure, Université PSL

WOW-284 predicted that minimum dual degree is controlled by the least distance eigenvalue in every connected graph of girth at least five. The paper gives exact counterexamples, identifies the shifted adjacency-spectrum mechanism behind the failure, proves structural restrictions on regular counterexamples, and develops a deletion theory for punctured Moore graphs.

The result at a glance

The disproof is short; the surrounding structure is not. The paper turns a distance-eigenvalue conjecture into a shifted adjacency-window problem, then studies the global, local, and perturbative consequences of that conversion.

Central regular mechanism

Diameter three converts WOW-284 into an open shifted spectral window.

For a connected \(k\)-regular graph of girth at least five and diameter three, strict failure is equivalent to every nonprincipal adjacency eigenvalue lying in the interval centered at \(-1\).

\[|\theta+1|<\sqrt{2k-2}.\]

One obstruction hierarchy

A single optimal slack operator connects the global linear-programming bound to integral order restrictions and local cycle constraints. The hierarchy explains why simply increasing the degree of the one-variable polynomial cannot settle the remaining graph-realizability questions.

What the paper proves

The claims below are deliberately separated by proof mode: analytic arguments, exact finite computations, and Lean kernel-checked certificates.

Counterexamples

Five exact orders

Explicit strict counterexamples at orders 38, 39, 40, 42, and 50, with exact graph, distance, dual-degree, and spectral certificates.

Structure

Degree and diameter restrictions

Every regular strict counterexample has degree at least six and diameter at most four; diameter four forces degree at least ten.

Optimization

Exact LP optimum and rigidity

The natural one-variable nonbacktracking LP has an exact optimum and a unique optimizer up to positive scale.

Integrality

Three-to-one quantization

The integral excess of the optimal slack matrix yields a strengthened universal order bound and a rigid arithmetic equality case.

Local theory

Cycle divisibility sieve

Two-by-two principal minors localize the spectral certificate into exact constraints on five-cycles through each edge.

Punctures

Exact Moore deletion spectra

One-vertex and two-vertex punctures admit complete invariant-subspace decompositions after distances are recomputed.

Stability

Uniform deletion bound

An incidence-Gram correction separates dual-degree loss from metric damage and gives configuration-sensitive stability estimates.

Formal methods

Lean and exact replay

The 50-vertex graph and selected finite spectra are kernel checked; the all-degree LP optimum and rigidity are formalized separately.

Three verification layers

The website makes the trust boundary visible. A finite verifier is not called a proof of a general theorem, and a matrix certificate is not presented as a full graph-level formalization unless the Lean development actually contains that bridge.
