# Writing and presentation guidance

Preserve the manuscript's dry, theorem-first mathematical style.

- State hypotheses before motivation and distinguish analytic proofs from exact computer-assisted classifications.
- Keep novelty language conservative: use “we derive” or “we prove,” never an unsupported priority claim.
- Preserve exact claim boundaries for Lean, Python certificates, finite classifications, and exploratory screens.
- Prefer short transition paragraphs that explain how a theorem advances one of the two proof hierarchies: optimal-slack obstructions or deletion stability.
- Use standard graph-theoretic and spectral terminology. Avoid rhetorical amplification and generic AI prose.
- Do not alter the canonical LaTeX manuscript through generated PreTeXt files; persistent website-only explanations belong in Paperforge insertions or the project landing pages.
