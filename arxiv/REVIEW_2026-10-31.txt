1. Five-cycles through an edge
------------------------------
In the closed-window edge-cycle proposition the lower bound remains

  sigma_uv >= 2k - 2.

Under the strict window the integral-slack theorem gives

  (g_k(A))_uv >= h_k + 1 = 6(k+2) + 1,

whereas the edge calculation gives

Subtracting yields the stronger strict-window consequence

  sigma_uv >= 2k - 1.

The added paragraph states the strict hypothesis explicitly. It does not
replace the weaker bound in the proposition that allows closed endpoints.

2. Strict six-cycle upper bound at order 50
------------------------------------------
For the degree-six, order-50 situation, the displayed moment bounds now read

  (43m^2 - 70200m + 119632500)/58500 <= N_6
      < (4220000 - 2200m - 7m^2)/2000.

The lower inequality remains weak. The upper inequality is strict because
the 3-by-3 localizing matrix L is positive definite, not merely semidefinite.

Diameter three makes I,A,A^2,A^3 linearly independent: evaluate a proposed
linear relation at vertex pairs of distances three, two, one and zero.
Since A is symmetric, it has at least four distinct eigenvalues. The
connected regular graph has a simple principal eigenvalue, so there are
at least three distinct shifted nonprincipal values y_i. The strict
spectral window gives 10-y_i^2 > 0. A nonzero polynomial of degree at most
two cannot vanish at all three values, so its positively weighted squared
evaluation sum is positive. This is the quadratic form of L, proving L > 0.
Consequently det L > 0 and the six-cycle upper bound is strict. No analogous
four-support argument has been proved for the larger moment matrix M, so
the lower bound correctly remains nonstrict.

The exact verifier now uses ceil(moment upper)-1 for the integer upper
endpoint and retains the weak lower endpoints. It confirms that the same
266 degree profiles survive. Only the possible integer moment endpoints
(m,N_6)=(0,2110) and (100,1965) are removed. This does not settle existence
of a degree-six order-50 counterexample. Both copies of the displayed bound

Local improvements implemented
------------------------------
1. Strict upper endpoint for five-cycle counts.
   Under the strict shifted adjacency window, the edge bounds now read
     2k-1 <= sigma_uv < U_(k,n),
     U_(k,n) = 2(k+2)^2(k^2+3)/n - 10k - 26.
   The new upper strictness follows from the existing Gram matrix. If the
   2x2 edge block with entries a,b had b=-a, positive semidefiniteness would
   put e_u+e_v in its full kernel. In the strict window this kernel is
   span(1) direct-sum ker(A+2I). Hence
     w=e_u+e_v-(2/n)1
   would satisfy Aw=-2w. The u-coordinate, for the edge uv, gives
     1-2k/n=-2+4/n,
   or n=2(k+2)/3<k+1 for k>=4, contradicting the elementary order bound for
   a simple k-regular graph. Thus b>-a. The coordinating reviewer checked
   this argument independently before adoption.

2. Carry both strict endpoints into the edge-cycle divisibility sieve.
   The closed-window statement is preserved. Under the strict window its
   strengthened integer bounds are
     ceil(max{2k-1, 2k^2-2k+2-n}) <= ceil(U_(k,n))-1.
   If they coincide at s, the same edge-incidence argument gives
     5 divides skn/2.
   The proof explicitly explains the ceil(U)-1 rule for an integral count.

3. State connectedness explicitly in the LP trace setup, aligning the local
   discussion with the graph-order theorem and the single principal
   eigendirection used by the trace argument.

4. Explain the rank distinction in the six-cycle moment appendix.
   Three distinct nonprincipal shifted eigenvalues and positive weights
